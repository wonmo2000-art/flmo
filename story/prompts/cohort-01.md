# 1기 — 2400년 · 열 명

> **입소 연령 12~15세.** 1기는 열 명이었다. 오백이 아니라 열 명.
> [`wall-and-training.md`](wall-and-training.md)은 20기(2590년)다. **이건 190년 전이다.**

## 1기가 20기와 다른 다섯 가지

**1. 열 명이다.**
오백 명이 줄 서 있는 것보다 **열 명이 거대한 홀에 있는 게 더 외롭다.**
20기는 붐비고, 1기는 텅 비어 있다.

**2. 아무도 결과를 모른다.**
의사들도 모른다. **이건 시술이 아니라 실험이다.**
20기의 어른들은 확신에 차 있고, 1기의 어른들은 서로 쳐다본다.

**3. 정화구가 조잡하다.**
190년 뒤에는 창백한 세라믹에 금테를 두른다. **지금은 놋쇠다.**
크고, 나사가 보이고, 배관이 밖으로 나와 있다. **고쳐 만든 티가 난다.**

**4. 제복이 없다.**
아직 제도가 없다. 아이들은 **집에서 입던 옷 그대로** 있다.
그래서 열 명이 전부 다르게 생겼다. 20기는 전부 똑같이 생겼다.

**5. 시설이 새것이다.**
콘크리트에 얼룩이 없고, 조명이 지나치게 밝고, 벽이 아프도록 하얗다.
**낡은 게 하나도 없어서 오히려 무섭다.**

> **1기는 밝고 깨끗하고 새것인데 열 명이 죽는다.**
> 20기는 낡고 붐비는데 아무도 안 죽는 것처럼 군다.

# 1부 · 도착

## C1-1 · 열 명 [기준 컷]

```
Extreme wide. A vast brand-new white concrete hall with a ceiling far overhead
and no windows, lit by hard overhead fixtures. Ten small figures stand loosely
together near the centre of the enormous empty floor, not in a line, not
arranged, just standing where they were left. Around them the hall runs away
empty in every direction. Two adults in clean white coats stand near a doorway
at the far side, talking to each other, not looking over.

Small adolescent build throughout, twelve to fifteen years old: heads large in
proportion to narrow shoulders, thin necks, unformed jawlines. They are still
wearing the clothes they arrived in — homespun wool, patched hide, knitted
things, all different colours, nothing matching. Nobody is in uniform. Several
are still holding small bundles.

Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. Hard overhead fixtures at 5000K,
over-lit, clean whites, brand-new concrete with no staining. Figures below one
fifteenth of frame height. Fine film grain. No text, no logo, no watermark.
```

> **열 명이 오백 명보다 외롭다.** 이 컷이 1기 전체다.
> 줄을 안 세웠다는 게 중요하다 — **아직 제도가 없다.**

Close on a small bundle held against the chest with both arms — a folded
knitted blanket tied with cord, a wooden cup pushed into the fold, a strip of
dried meat. Thin young hands with bitten nails gripping it. The sleeves are
homespun wool, worn soft. Behind, out of focus, brand-new white concrete floor
running away empty.

Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. Hard overhead light at 5000K, shallow

> **아무도 짐을 가져가라고 안 했다.** 그래서 다들 뭔가 들고 왔다.
> 20기에는 이 컷이 없다. 190년 사이에 그것도 규정이 됐다.

Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. Hard overhead fixtures at 5000K,

# 2부 · 시술

## C1-4 · 놋쇠 [정화구 1세대]

```
Extreme close on a crude brass port newly set into the base of a narrow young
throat — a raised circular fitting the width of two fingers, machined visibly,
with four exposed screws around its rim and a short length of rubber tubing
still clamped into its side and running out of frame. The brass is bright and
unworn. The skin around it is red and freshly closed, held with visible
stitches. Adult gloved hands are adjusting the clamp and look large against the
neck.

Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. One hard overhead light at 5000K,
shallow focus. Real brass with machining marks, real rubber, real skin texture
with visible pores. No beauty retouching. Fine film grain. No text, no logo, no
watermark.
```

> **190년 뒤에는 이게 창백한 세라믹에 금테가 된다.** 지금은 나사가 보인다.
> **고쳐 만든 티가 나야 한다.** 처음이니까.

## C1-5 · 실험

```
A brand-new white concrete room with a single reclining chair at its centre and
nothing else in it. One small figure lies back in the chair, awake, the collar
of a homespun shirt folded open, a brass port at the throat with rubber tubing
running from it up to a bank of glass vessels on a wheeled trolley. The chair
is built for an adult and much too big — the feet do not reach the end of it.

Four adults in clean white coats stand around the chair at full adult height.
Three are watching the glass vessels. One is watching the face. Nobody is
touching anything.

Twelve to fifteen years old: head large in proportion to narrow shoulders, thin
neck and wrists, an unformed jawline. Awake and calm, looking at the ceiling.

Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. Hard overhead fixtures at 5000K,
over-lit, clean whites, brand-new concrete. Fine film grain. No text, no logo,
no watermark.
```

> **네 명이 지켜보고 아무도 아무것도 안 한다.** 그게 실험이다.
> **한 명만 얼굴을 본다.** 그 사람이 나중에 무너진다.

## C1-6 · 빈 의자

```
A brand-new white concrete room with a single reclining chair at its centre,
empty, the seat still holding the shape of a small body. A brass port fitting
lies on a tray beside it with a short length of cut rubber tubing. A bank of
glass vessels on a wheeled trolley stands unattended, the vessels clear and
dry. Nobody is in the room.

Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. Hard overhead fixtures at 5000K,
over-lit, clean whites, brand-new concrete with no staining. Fine film grain.
No text, no logo, no watermark.

> **정화구를 회수한다.** 아직 귀한 물건이라서.
> 시체는 안 보여준다. **뽑아낸 부품 하나면 충분하다.**

```
Extreme wide. The same vast brand-new white concrete hall. Nine small figures
stand loosely near the centre of the enormous empty floor in mismatched
homespun clothes, all of them looking in the same direction at a doorway. The
tenth space is simply not occupied. Nothing marks it. Two adults in white coats

Small adolescent build throughout, twelve to fifteen years old: heads large in

Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. Hard overhead fixtures at 5000K,

> **C1-1과 완전히 같은 구도로 뽑는다.** 한 명만 없다.
> 이 두 컷을 나란히 놓는 게 1기의 전부다.

# 3부 · 여울

## C1-8 · 안 열린다

```
Close on a small figure sitting upright in a reclining chair, seen from the
chest up, the collar of a homespun shirt folded open. A crude brass port sits
at the base of the throat with rubber tubing clamped into it, and the tubing is
completely clear and empty. The face is calm and looking straight ahead at
nothing. Out of focus behind, two adults in white coats have turned to each
other rather than to the chair.

Twelve to fifteen years old: head large in proportion to narrow shoulders, thin
neck, an unformed jawline with soft rounded cheeks, blunt dark hair cut at the
jaw. The skin is entirely unmarked apart from the fresh line around the port.

Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. Hard overhead light at 5000K, shallow
focus. Real brass, real rubber, real skin texture. No beauty retouching. Fine
film grain. No text, no logo, no watermark.
```

> **관이 비어 있다.** 아무것도 안 나왔다. 불량품이다.
> **어른 둘이 아이가 아니라 서로를 본다.** 그게 판정이다.

## C1-9 · 이름

```
Two small figures sitting side by side on the floor with their backs against a
brand-new white concrete wall in an empty corridor, knees up, in mismatched
homespun clothes. One is drawing something on the floor with a fingertip. The
other is watching the finger. Both have crude brass ports at the base of the
throat, one with tubing still attached. The corridor is enormous and empty in
both directions and its ceiling is far above them.

Twelve to fifteen years old: heads large in proportion to narrow shoulders,
thin wrists on the knees, unformed jawlines.

Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. Hard overhead fixtures at 5000K,
over-lit, clean whites. Fine film grain. No text, no logo, no watermark.
```

> **여울에게 이름을 지어준 순간이다.** 번호가 없어서 부를 방법이 없었으니까.
> 바닥에 손가락으로 쓴다. **글자는 안 보여준다.**

## C1-10 · 190년 뒤 [짝 컷]

```
The same corridor, now old. The concrete is stained, patched and darkened at
the corners, the lighting reduced to one dim continuous strip, the whites gone
grey. A woman who looks about twenty is mopping the floor alone in a worn grey
work uniform with no markings. Her skin is entirely unmarked — no scarring, no
ports, no discolouration. She has stopped moving and is looking at one
particular spot on the floor near the wall.

Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. Single dim ceiling strip at 4000K,
shadowless. Aged stained grey concrete. Real skin texture with visible pores.
No beauty retouching. Fine film grain. No text, no logo, no watermark.
```

> **C1-9와 완전히 같은 복도, 같은 각도로 뽑는다.**
> 복도만 190년 늙었다. **여울은 안 늙었다.**
> 그리고 여울이 보고 있는 바닥이 **손가락으로 글자를 썼던 자리**다.

## 톤 규칙 하나

**1기 파트는 밝다.** 새 콘크리트, 과한 조명, 흰색.
20기 파트는 어둡다. 낡고, 조명이 줄고, 회색이다.

> **처음이 제일 밝았다.** 그게 이 파트의 잔인함이다.
