1 — 정착지와 넘어오는 것

1A

여명 직전의 카란

부감 극와이드. 수백 동의 천막이 원을 그리고, 지평선에 거대한 달.

SCENE CONTEXT
A nomad settlement of several hundred tents sits in a basin between enormous
dunes in the blue minutes before dawn. Nothing has happened yet.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@karan — controls tents, banners, animal pens, sand and dune geography only;
reference framing not inherited.
@moon — controls the size and surface of the low moon only; it sits on the
horizon line.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: the near dune crest, wind-carved, running diagonally across the
bottom of frame. Midground: the basin floor, several hundred dark tents laid
out in concentric rings around one larger central tent, rope banners on poles,
low fenced pens with camels standing in them, three cooking fires still burning
small and orange. Background: dune ridges stepping back to a flat horizon, and
one enormous moon sitting directly on that horizon, wider than the whole
settlement. Camera is high above the near crest looking down and across.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
High wide from 60 metres above the near dune crest, tilted down 30 degrees. The
settlement fills the lower two thirds, the moon fills the right third of the
sky and touches the horizon line. Nothing moves except banners and smoke.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot. The camera does not cut on its own. It holds longest at
the open and only begins moving in the final third.

OPTICS
84° FOV for the entire shot, rectilinear, one lens with no change.

CAMERA
Aerial, holding still at 60 metres for the first half, then drifting forward
over the crest at 2 m/s and descending 10 metres. Micro-corrections of 5 to 10
cm so it reads as flown. Focus holds on the settlement throughout.

ACTION
Rope banners snap steadily on their poles. Thin smoke rises from three fires
and bends sideways in the wind. Camels shift in the pens. One figure crosses
between two tents carrying a bucket and does not look up. The moon does not
move. Everything in frame is calm and continues being calm for the entire shot.

PHYSICS
Real cloth weight in the tent walls, which breathe in and out with the wind.
Sand streams off the near crest in thin ribbons. Smoke behaves as smoke in
moving air.

LIGHTING
Pre-dawn ambient at 8500K, no sun, exposed 2 stops under. The moon is the
brightest thing in frame by a wide margin. Three small 2000K fire points in the
basin. Haze at 20%.

AUDIO
Wind across open sand, rope banners snapping, camels shifting, one distant
animal call. Underneath everything, a low unpitched rumble at the edge of
hearing, somewhere like distant ground movement. No
music. No dialogue.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action, 4K, large-format detail, natural desaturated colour,
fine grain, real-time.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The moon stays on the horizon line at the same size for the entire shot.
Tents stay in concentric rings and hold their positions.
The settlement stays calm from the first frame to the last.
This clip is one fragment of a longer film — end mid-drift, no settling shot.
1B

능선을 넘어오는 것

보라색 연기가 소리 없이 사구를 넘어 쏟아진다.

SCENE CONTEXT
A wall of violet vapour comes over a dune ridge above a sleeping nomad
settlement and begins pouring down the slope toward it. It makes no sound.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@karan — controls tents, banners, pens, sand and dune geography only; reference
framing not inherited.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: the basin floor with the outermost ring of dark tents, banners on
poles. Midground: the long sand slope rising away from them. Background: the
dune ridge line against a pre-dawn sky, and beyond it the violet mass cresting.
Camera sits low in the basin looking up the slope.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Low wide, camera 1.5 metres above the sand, 40 metres out from the nearest
tents, looking up the slope at the ridge 200 metres away. Two tent peaks in the
lower corners for scale. The ridge is clean and empty for the first moment.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot. The camera does not cut on its own. The empty ridge holds
longest; the pour is fastest.

OPTICS
63° FOV for the entire shot, rectilinear, one lens with no change.

CAMERA
Locked off and completely still for the first half, then a slow 1 m/s push in
toward the slope. No tilt, no pan.

ACTION
The ridge line stays empty and still. Then violet vapour rises over it along
its whole length at once and comes down the face of the dune — not blowing, not
drifting, but pouring, the way water pours over sand, keeping a defined leading
edge that runs downhill faster than the body behind it. It is completely
silent. Sand does not lift in front of it. Banners in the foreground keep
snapping in a wind that the vapour is clearly not part of.

PHYSICS
The vapour has weight. It hugs the slope, fills the hollows first and takes the
high ground last, and its leading edge stays sharp instead of feathering. It
moves at a steady 4 m/s regardless of the wind direction on the banners.

LIGHTING
Pre-dawn ambient at 8500K, exposed 2 stops under. The vapour is self-luminous
at very low level, a dull violet that lifts the sand it passes over by half a
stop. Haze at 20% rising to 45% as it arrives.

AUDIO
Wind, banners, sand. The vapour itself makes no sound at all. The very low
continuous tone underneath everything grows slightly louder. No music. No
dialogue.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action, 4K, large-format detail, natural desaturated colour with
one violet source, fine grain, real-time.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The vapour stays silent for the entire shot.
Its leading edge stays sharp and defined and keeps contact with the sand.
The camera stays locked and then pushes in on one axis only.
This clip is one fragment of a longer film — end mid-pour, no settling shot.
2A

짐승들이 먼저 안다

낙타가 줄을 끊고, 염소 떼가 천막 사이를 뚫고, 새 떼가 터져 오른다.

SCENE CONTEXT
The animals in a nomad settlement panic all at once, seconds before any person
reacts. Violet vapour is arriving at the outer tents behind them.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@karan — controls tents, banners, pens, sand and dune geography only; reference
framing not inherited.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: a low fenced pen of tethered camels on churned sand. Midground: a
lane running between two rows of dark tents, banners on poles. Background: the
outer ring of tents with violet vapour already at their bases and rising up
their walls. Camera is in the lane at chest height.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Medium wide, camera 1.4 metres high in the lane, 8 metres from the camel pen at
frame left. Twelve camels stand tethered and still. The lane runs away from
camera toward the outer tents where the violet edge is just visible. No people
in frame.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot. The camera does not cut on its own. The stillness at the
open is brief; everything after it is fast.

OPTICS
63° FOV for the entire shot, rectilinear, one lens with no change.

CAMERA
Handheld at 1.4 metres with 3 cm tremor, backing away down the lane at 6 km/h
as the animals come toward it, then swinging up 40 degrees at the end. Focus
rides the nearest animals.

ACTION
Every camel lifts its head at the same instant. They pull back on their tethers
together, the ropes go taut, and three of them snap — the animals come out of
the pen sideways, knocking the fence rail down. WHILE that is happening a herd
of goats bursts through the lane from behind camera and runs the length of it
between the tents, pouring around the camera. WHILE the goats are still passing,
a mass of birds comes off the tent peaks and off the ground all at once and
goes straight up out of frame. All three are overlapping and continuous. Not one
person appears.

PERFORMANCE
The animals do not look at the vapour. They look away from it and move away from
it, which is what makes it read.

PHYSICS
Real animal weight and real herd behaviour — the goats break around obstacles
rather than through them, camels move with their heads high and their gait
wrong. Sand kicks up in sheets. The fence rail falls with real mass. Rope goes
taut, stretches, then parts.

LIGHTING
Pre-dawn ambient at 8500K, exposed 2 stops under, with a dull violet lift on
the outer tents in the background. Haze at 45%.

AUDIO
Rope creaking then snapping, hooves on sand, camels calling, goats, a wall of
wings leaving all at once, a fence rail hitting the ground. The violet vapour
still makes no sound. Underneath it, the low unpitched rumble. Every sound is unpitched and textural
— no tones, no drones, no instruments. No dialogue.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action, 4K, large-format detail, natural desaturated colour, fine
grain, real-time.

POSITIVE LOCKS
No people appear in this shot.
All three animal actions overlap and none of them waits its turn.
The vapour stays silent and stays in the background.
This clip is one fragment of a longer film — end mid-stampede, no settling
shot.
2B

옷만 남는다

양탄자를 걷던 노인이 연기에 잠긴다. 비명이 없다.

SCENE CONTEXT
An elder rolling a rug outside a tent is overtaken by silent violet vapour. When
it has passed, only the clothes are lying on the sand.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@elder — 100% matches the reference; dark brown robe, rope belt, head wrap,

@karan — controls tents, drying rack, sand and props only; reference framing not

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: a woven rug half rolled on the sand and a wooden drying rack hung
with cloth. Midground: the side wall of a dark tent, guy ropes staked out.
Background: the lane between tents, filling with violet. Camera is low and close
on the rug.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Low medium, camera 0.6 metres above the sand, 3 metres from the rug, looking
slightly up. The elder is bent over the rug with both hands on it, back to the
lane. The drying rack stands 2 metres behind at frame right. Violet is visible
in the depth of the lane but has not arrived.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot. The camera does not cut on its own. It holds absolutely
still for the entire shot.

OPTICS
47° FOV for the entire shot, rectilinear, one lens with no change.

CAMERA
Locked off on a low tripod. It does not move, tilt, pan or push at any point.

ACTION
The elder keeps rolling the rug, working steadily, and lifts one hand to
straighten the head wrap. The violet arrives from the lane and crosses the frame
from right to left at a steady walking pace, taking the drying rack first, then
the tent wall, then the elder. There is no reaction, no turn, no cry — the
figure is simply inside it and then the vapour has moved on past frame left. The
rug is still half rolled. Under the drying rack, the dark brown robe, the rope
belt and the head wrap are lying on the sand in the shape they fell in. The
sand around them is undisturbed.

PHYSICS
The vapour keeps a sharp leading edge and hugs the ground. The cloth on the
drying rack lifts as it passes and settles again. The fallen robe has real
weight and real folds, and it does not smoke, burn, or move.

LIGHTING
Pre-dawn ambient at 8500K, exposed 2 stops under, with a dull violet self-glow
that lifts the sand half a stop as it crosses and takes the light with it when
it leaves. Haze at 45%.

AUDIO
Wind, cloth on the drying rack, the dry sound of a rug being rolled — and then
nothing at all where a voice should be. The vapour is silent. The low unpitched rumble
continues. No music. No dialogue.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action, 4K, large-format detail, natural desaturated colour with
one violet source, fine grain, real-time. Impact conveyed entirely through
absence, sound and the empty clothing.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The camera stays locked and still for the entire shot.
The vapour crosses at a steady walking pace and stays silent.
After it passes, the clothing lies on undisturbed sand.
This clip is one fragment of a longer film — end holding on the empty robe, no
settling shot.

2 — 그것이 보인다

3A

천막 위의 실루엣

가장 큰 천막의 다섯 배. 다리가 너무 길고 너무 느리다.

SCENE CONTEXT
Something enormous walks through the violet vapour above the roofline of a
nomad settlement. Only its silhouette is legible.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@giants — 100% matches the reference for proportion and surface; reference
framing NOT inherited. Never more than three in frame.
@karan — controls tent peaks, poles and banners only; reference framing not
inherited.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: two tent poles rising steeply into frame from the bottom corners,
dark and out of focus, framing the centre. Midground: a field of tent peaks and
guy ropes, half drowned in violet. Background: the vapour, thick and even, with
tall pale shapes standing in it. Camera is on the ground looking steeply up
between the poles.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Low angle, camera 0.5 metres above the sand, tilted up 45 degrees, framed
between two foreground tent poles that occupy the left and right thirds. Three
tall pale figures stand in the vapour beyond the tent peaks — the nearest at 80
metres, the second at 140 metres, the third at 220 metres. The nearest one
stands five times the height of the largest tent; a person's head would not
reach the top of its shin.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot. The camera does not cut on its own. It holds longest of any
shot in the scene.

OPTICS
63° FOV for the entire shot, rectilinear, one lens with no change.

CAMERA
Locked off, low, tilted up. It does not move at any point.

ACTION
The nearest figure takes two steps across the frame in the whole shot. Each leg
lifts, swings and sets down far more slowly than a body that size would need to,
and the sand does not shake when the foot lands. The other two do not move at
all. The vapour drifts continuously in front of and behind all three so their
edges keep dissolving and reforming. Banners in the midground keep snapping the
whole time.

PHYSICS
The figures displace the vapour as they pass through it. The ground does not
respond to their weight in any way — no shake, no impact, no sand thrown. That
mismatch is the point and holds for the entire shot.

LIGHTING
Pre-dawn ambient at 8500K, exposed 2 stops under. The vapour is a dull
self-luminous violet and the figures read paler than it, lit from within the
vapour rather than from the sky. Haze at 70%.

AUDIO
A low unpitched rumble is loudest here and clearly coming from the tall figures,
swelling and fading slowly like moving earth. Wind and banners underneath. No
footsteps. Every sound is unpitched and textural — no tones, no drones, no
instruments, no singing. No dialogue.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action, 4K, large-format detail, natural desaturated colour with
one violet source, fine grain, real-time.

POSITIVE LOCKS
EXACTLY THREE tall figures in the entire generation.
The nearest stands five times the height of the largest tent; a person's head
would not reach the top of its shin.
The ground stays completely still under them for the entire shot.
They stay silhouetted and half dissolved in vapour — no clean full reveal.
This clip is one fragment of a longer film — end mid-step, no settling shot.

4A

달린다

세례가 포대기를 안고 천막 사이를 달린다. 맨발이 모래에 빠진다.

SCENE CONTEXT
A woman runs through the lanes of a nomad settlement carrying a wrapped infant,
away from the violet vapour behind her.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@sere — 100% matches the reference; face, long dark hair now loose and moving,
layered cream and undyed linen wraps. Studio background and layout NOT
inherited. She is the only person in this generation.
@karan — controls tents, guy ropes, banners and sand only; reference framing not
inherited.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: sand churned by running feet, guy ropes staked low and crossing the
path. Midground: a narrow lane between dark tent walls that breathe in the wind.
Background: behind her, the lane filling with violet; ahead of her, the lane
opening onto the base of a dune. Camera is low and running ahead of her.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Low medium, camera 0.9 metres above the sand, 3 metres ahead of her and backing
up. She is centre frame, barefoot, running toward camera with a wrapped bundle
held high against her chest in both arms. The violet is 25 metres behind her in
the lane and closing. Her hair is loose and moving.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot. The camera does not cut on its own. It runs at one speed
from first frame to last.

OPTICS
63° FOV for the entire shot, rectilinear, one lens with no change. High shutter
angle so sand grains, cloth edges and her hands stay crisply resolved.

CAMERA
Handheld, 0.9 metres high, backing away ahead of her at 13 km/h with 4 cm
tremor, dropping to 0.4 metres for two seconds mid-shot to catch the feet, then
rising back. Focus rides the bundle.

ACTION
She runs with her weight forward and both arms locked around the bundle, so her
balance is wrong and she has to fight for every step. Her bare feet sink to the
ankle in the loose sand and she loses ground on each push. She turns her
shoulder to clear a tent guy rope without slowing. She does not look back, not
once, at any point in the shot. WHILE she runs, the violet keeps coming down the
lane behind her at a steady pace and the tent walls keep breathing in the wind.

PERFORMANCE
Her jaw is set and her breathing is loud and ragged. Her eyes are fixed forward
on the dune. Her hands keep adjusting their grip on the bundle without her
looking down. There is no screaming and no crying.

PHYSICS
Real weight of a carried infant against her chest, which changes how she runs.
Sand collapses under every barefoot step and throws back behind her. Loose hair
and linen wraps trail behind the direction of travel. Guy ropes flex when her
shoulder brushes them.

LIGHTING
Pre-dawn ambient at 8500K, exposed 2 stops under. A dull violet self-glow behind
her lifts her back and shoulders half a stop while her face stays in the cooler
ambient. Haze at 45%.

AUDIO
Bare feet in deep sand, ragged breathing, linen moving, a single infant cry
close to the microphone, tent cloth in the wind. The vapour is silent. The very
low tone continues behind everything. No music. No dialogue.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action, 4K, large-format detail, natural desaturated colour, fine
grain, real-time.

POSITIVE LOCKS
EXACTLY ONE person in the entire generation, identical face, hair and wraps from
first frame to last.
She keeps both arms around the bundle at all times and stays barefoot.
She faces forward for the entire shot.
This clip is one fragment of a longer film — end mid-stride, no settling shot.

A woman carrying a wrapped infant climbs out of a settlement onto a dune face,

and undyed linen wraps. Studio background and layout NOT inherited. She is the
only person in this generation.
@karan — controls the outermost tents and sand only; reference framing not
inherited.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: the lower dune face, loose sand giving way under weight. Midground:
the last row of tents at the settlement edge. Background: an unbroken wall of
violet standing vertically behind those tents, from the sand to well above the
tent peaks. Camera is up the dune ahead of her, looking back down past her.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Wide, camera 2 metres above the dune face and 12 metres up-slope from her,

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot. The camera does not cut on its own. The climb is fast; the

OPTICS
63° FOV for the entire shot, rectilinear, one lens with no change.

PHYSICS
Sand collapses under every step and runs back down behind her. The vertical

cloth in front of it still moves in the wind, so the air is clearly still
moving even though the wall is not.

LIGHTING
Pre-dawn ambient at 8500K, exposed 2 stops under. The violet wall is the
brightest thing in the lower frame and lifts the sand in front of it by a stop.
Her face stays in cooler ambient. Haze at 70% behind the wall, 30% in front.

AUDIO
Bare feet in collapsing sand, ragged breathing, linen, wind over the crest. At

away for two seconds. The low unpitched rumble holds a single sustained note. No music.
No dialogue.

Photoreal live-action, 4K, large-format detail, natural desaturated colour with

이 장면의 규칙

연기는 소리가 없다. 이 장면의 모든 공포가 거기서 나온다. 짐승 소리, 바람 소리, 천 소리는 계속 나는데 다가오는 것만 조용하다.

비명이 없다. 노인은 소리 없이 사라지고 옷만 남는다. 보여주지 않는 게 더 세다.

땅이 안 흔들린다. 거인이 그만한 크기로 걷는데 모래가 반응하지 않는다. 그 어긋남이 저것이 무엇인지 말한다.

따뜻한 색은 두 번만 나온다. 화살통 안의 금빛, 그리고 마지막 불의 고리. 그 사이는 전부 8500K 푸른빛이다.

불의 고리는 달과 같은 지름이어야 한다. 이 각운이 이 장면의 끝이다.
