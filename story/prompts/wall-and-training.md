## 이 세트의 규칙 다섯

**1. 학대가 안 보여야 한다.**
아이들은 깨끗하고, 잘 먹었고, 소중히 관리된다. **오래 쓰기 위해서다.**
피가 없다. 쇠사슬이 없다. 우는 얼굴이 없다. 그래서 더 무섭다.

**2. 사람이 작아야 한다.**
공간이 주인공이다. 극와이드에서 인물은 **프레임 높이의 10분의 1 이하.**

**3. 빛이 하나다.**
위에서 내려오는 그림자 없는 조명, 아니면 아주 높은 창 하나.
따뜻한 색이 벽 안에는 없다.

**4. 화려할수록 많이 쓰인 것이다.**
등급이 오르면 정화구가 늘고 금테가 둘린다. **예쁜 애가 제일 망가진 애다.**

**5. 조용하다.**
오백 명이 있는데 소리가 안 난다. 이게 이 시설의 성격이다.


## 나이 블록 — 아이가 어른으로 나오는 문제

이미지 모델은 **숫자를 잘 안 듣는다.** "12세"라고 써도 성인 얼굴이 나온다.
듣는 건 **비율 · 옷 · 옆에 선 어른** 셋이다. 아래를 통째로 붙인다.

```
Small adolescent build throughout, twelve to fifteen years old: heads large in
proportion to narrow shoulders, thin necks and wrists, unformed jawlines with
soft rounded cheeks, fine soft hair. The uniforms were cut for adults and taken
in — sleeves turned back at the cuff, trousers gathered and belted high,
collars standing away from the neck. The tallest of them reaches an adult's
shoulder.
```

**그리고 프레임에 어른을 하나 넣는다.** 이게 제일 강력하다.
비교 대상이 없으면 모델은 무조건 성인으로 그린다.

```
One adult in clean grey staff uniform stands among them at full adult height
for scale.
```

> **잘 안 될 때 순서대로 시도한다**
> 1. 어른을 프레임에 넣는다 ← **이거 하나로 대부분 해결된다**
> 2. 소매를 접고 바지를 걷은 걸 명시한다 — 안 맞는 옷이 나이를 만든다
> 3. `heads large in proportion to narrow shoulders` 를 앞쪽으로 옮긴다
> 4. 얼굴 클로즈업은 **어깨까지 넣는다.** 얼굴만 크게 잡으면 무조건 어른이 된다

## 피폭 표현 — 아이한테는 약하게

**입소 직후의 아이들은 아직 안 망가졌다.** 이게 설정상으로도 맞다.
어린 몸에 켈로이드를 두껍게 그리면 그림이 흉해지고 모델도 거부한다.

|---|---|

| **오래 쓰인 몸** | 켈로이드, 자반, 탈모 — 완성형 세하나 집행자에게만 |

```
each ceramic port, small dark bruising on the forearms, thickened ridged nails,
skin thin enough that veins show at the temple. They are clean, well-fed and
carefully kept. The damage is not neglect. It is use.
```

# 4부 · 10초 영상 · 첫날

> 스틸 말고 움직이는 컷이 필요하면 이거 하나부터.
> 훈련이 아니라 **입소 첫날 벽을 넘는 순간**이다.

```
SCENE CONTEXT
A steel platform carrying twenty new arrivals rises up the outside of an
enormous white wall, crests the rim, and swings inward over the compound. They
see what is on the other side for the first time.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@wall — controls the wall surface, scale and the compound layout only;
reference framing not inherited.
@intake — controls the look of the arrivals and their uniforms only; the sheet
holds several distinct individuals and supplies the whole group.

LOCATION MAP
The wall face fills the frame at the open — unbroken white, no seams, no
openings. Beyond the rim, a vast enclosed grey concrete compound: flat
courtyards, long low blocks, drainage channels, all the same poured grey, the
far side closed by more wall. Below and behind, a bleached dust plain with
three small trucks and a scatter of charred bare tree trunks. Flat
overcast-filtered daylight at 5600K outside, cooling to 4000K over the
compound.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
The camera is level with the platform and 6 metres out from the wall face.
Twenty small figures in stiff new homemade clothes stand packed on the steel
platform, gripping the rail, all facing outward and down. Two adults in clean
grey staff uniforms stand at the back at full adult height, and the tallest of
the others reaches their shoulder. The wall fills the entire background as one
white surface with no feature on it. The ground is already 40 metres below.

Everyone on the platform except the two staff is twelve to fifteen years old:
heads large in proportion to narrow shoulders, thin necks and wrists, unformed
jawlines. Their clothes are too big — sleeves turned back at the cuff, trousers
gathered and belted high, collars standing away from the neck.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot. The camera does not cut on its own. The climb holds
longest, the crest turns fastest, the last look holds again.

OPTICS
84° FOV for the entire shot, rectilinear, one lens with no change at any point.

CAMERA
One unbroken aerial move. It rises with the platform at 3 m/s, 6 metres out
from the wall face, holding the platform at frame left and the blank white
surface filling everything else. As the platform reaches the rim the camera
arcs over and around it, crossing the top of the wall, and settles looking down
and inward across the compound with the platform now small in the lower corner.
Micro-corrections of 5 to 10 cm throughout so it reads as a flown machine.

ACTION
The platform rises steadily with the cables singing under load. The arrivals
keep their weight on the rail and their eyes on the ground falling away below.
Two of them look up at the rim as it approaches. As the platform crests, wind
catches everyone's clothes at once, and the whole group turns inward together —
the movement passes across the platform like a wave rather than all at once.
Then nobody moves at all. One figure at the rail edge does not turn with the
others; she is already looking down at the compound, and she stays that way as
the camera pulls up and away.

PERFORMANCE
No panic and no crying. The reactions read as arriving somewhere they were told
was good: shoulders up, chins lifting, one grin further down the rail, one
bouncing on the balls of the feet. The stillness that follows the turn is the
only tell. The two staff do not react at all.

PHYSICS
Steel platform swaying 10 to 15 cm on its cables, cables under real tension and
vibrating, clothing and hair pulled hard sideways at the rim where the wind
crosses. Grip pressure visible in the hands on the rail. Real weight in the
whole assembly.

LIGHTING
Flat overcast-filtered daylight at 5600K from an even sky, no visible sun, no
shadow direction on the white wall face. The compound below sits a stop darker
and cooler at 4000K. Dust haze at 25% on the plain, none on the wall.

AUDIO
Wind rising steadily with altitude, cable tension creaking, the platform's
steel deck flexing under shifting weight, cloth snapping at the rim, one
distant motor working far above. Twenty people breathing and nobody speaking.
No dialogue. No music.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action, 4K, large-format cinema detail, physically real
materials, natural desaturated colour, fine grain, real-time throughout.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The wall stays completely blank — one continuous white surface with no door, no
window, no stair and no opening anywhere in the shot.
Nothing connects the top of the wall to the compound floor except the cables.
EXACTLY ONE platform, carrying the same group from the first frame to the last.
Everyone on it except the two staff stays small in build, with heads large in
proportion to narrow shoulders and clothes visibly cut for someone bigger.
The compound below stays grey, geometric and empty of people.
Figures stay under one twentieth of frame height once the camera is over the
rim.
This clip is one fragment of a longer film — end mid-move with the camera still
rising, no settling shot.
```
