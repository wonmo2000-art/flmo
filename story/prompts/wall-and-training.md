## 이 세트의 규칙 다섯

**1. 학대가 안 보여야 한다.**
아이들은 깨끗하고, 잘 먹었고, 소중히 관리된다. **오래 쓰기 위해서다.**
피가 없다. 쇠사슬이 없다. 우는 얼굴이 없다. 그래서 더 무섭다.

**2. 사람이 작아야 한다.**
공간이 주인공이다. 극와이드에서 인물은 **프레임 높이의 10분의 1 이하.**

**3. 빛이 하나다.**
위에서 내려오는 그림자 없는 조명, 아니면 아주 높은 창 하나.
따뜻한 색이 벽 안에는 없다.

**4. 화려할수록 많이 쓰인 것이다.**
등급이 오르면 정화구가 늘고 금테가 둘린다. **예쁜 애가 제일 망가진 애다.**

**5. 조용하다.**
오백 명이 있는데 소리가 안 난다. 이게 이 시설의 성격이다.

## 공통 블록

```
Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. Single overhead light source at 4000K,
soft and shadowless, exposed 1 stop under. Grey poured concrete, pale ceramic,
matte silver. No warm colour anywhere. Deep clean blacks in the depth of the
frame. Real skin texture with visible pores. No airbrushing, no illustration,
no painterly rendering, no CGI sheen, no beauty retouching.
Fine film grain. No text, no logo, no watermark.
```

**피폭 블록** — 몸이 보이는 컷에는 반드시 붙인다.

```
Visible radiation damage, medically grounded and never decorative: irregular
patches of hair loss, mottled depigmentation, raised keloid scarring ringing
each ceramic port, small dark bruising on the forearms, thickened ridged nails,
skin thin enough that veins show at the temple. They are clean, well-fed and
carefully kept. The damage is not neglect. It is use.
```

# 1부 · 벽

## W-1 · 벽 [기준 컷]

```
Extreme wide. A featureless white wall runs across the entire frame from edge
to edge through a flat bleached dust plain, 60 metres tall, with no door, no
window and no visible opening anywhere along its length. It continues past both
edges of the frame with no end in sight. Three small cargo trucks are stopped
at its base and are dwarfed to almost nothing. A crowd stands beside them
looking up. The wall is the only clean surface anywhere in the landscape.

Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. Flat overcast-filtered midday at
5600K. Bleached earth palette against unbroken white. Dust haze at 25% on
everything except the wall, which stays sharp. Figures below one twentieth of
frame height. Fine film grain. No text, no logo, no watermark.
```

> **먼지 속에서 유일하게 깨끗한 것.** 이 하나가 벽의 성격을 다 말한다.

## W-2 · 크레인

```
Low angle from the ground looking up the face of an enormous white wall that
fills the frame. Far above, a steel crane arm has swung out over the top edge
and a flat steel platform hangs from cables, descending toward camera. The
platform is empty. Everything else in frame is unbroken white. The scale is
readable only from the cables.

Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. Flat overcast midday at 5600K. Real
oxidised steel and cable against clean white surface. Fine film grain. No text,
no logo, no watermark.
```

## W-3 · 승강대

```
A flat steel platform rising on cables against an enormous unbroken white wall,
seen from slightly below and to the side. Twenty figures stand packed on it in
stiff new clothes, gripping the rail, all of them looking outward and down.
Below them the dust plain and three small trucks are already far away. Nothing
above them but white surface running out of frame.

Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. Flat overcast midday at 5600K. Extreme
vertical scale. Bleached earth far below, unbroken white behind. Fine film

```

## W-4 · 넘어가는 순간 [최대 컷]

```
From the top edge of an enormous white wall, looking down and inward. A steel
platform crests the rim in the near foreground with figures on it, small. Below
and beyond, a vast enclosed grey concrete compound opens up — flat courtyards,
long low blocks, geometric drainage channels, everything the same poured grey,
laid out to the far side where the wall closes the circle again. There are no
stairs anywhere. There is no ramp. There is no gate. Nothing connects the top
of the wall to the ground except the cables.

Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. Flat overcast daylight at 5600K
outside, falling to a cooler 4000K in the compound below. Bleached white rim,
grey interior. Extreme scale, figures below one thirtieth of frame height. Fine
film grain. No text, no logo, no watermark.
```

> **"계단이 없네."** 이 컷은 그 한 줄을 위해 존재한다.

# 4부 · 10초 영상 · 첫날

> 스틸 말고 움직이는 컷이 필요하면 이거 하나부터.
> 훈련이 아니라 **입소 첫날 벽을 넘는 순간**이다.

```
SCENE CONTEXT
A steel platform carrying twenty new arrivals rises up the outside of an
enormous white wall, crests the rim, and swings inward over the compound. They
see what is on the other side for the first time.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@wall — controls the wall surface, scale and the compound layout only;
reference framing not inherited.
@intake — controls the look of the arrivals and their uniforms only; the sheet
holds several distinct individuals and supplies the whole group.

LOCATION MAP
The wall face fills the frame at the open — unbroken white, no seams, no
openings. Beyond the rim, a vast enclosed grey concrete compound: flat
courtyards, long low blocks, drainage channels, all the same poured grey, the
far side closed by more wall. Below and behind, a bleached dust plain with
three small trucks and a scatter of charred bare tree trunks. Flat
overcast-filtered daylight at 5600K outside, cooling to 4000K over the
compound.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
The camera is level with the platform and 6 metres out from the wall face.
Twenty figures in stiff new clothes stand packed on the steel platform,
gripping the rail, all facing outward and down. The wall fills the entire
background as one white surface with no feature on it. The ground is already
40 metres below.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot. The camera does not cut on its own. The climb holds
longest, the crest turns fastest, the last look holds again.

OPTICS
84° FOV for the entire shot, rectilinear, one lens with no change at any point.

CAMERA
One unbroken aerial move. It rises with the platform at 3 m/s, 6 metres out
from the wall face, holding the platform at frame left and the blank white
surface filling everything else. As the platform reaches the rim the camera
arcs over and around it, crossing the top of the wall, and settles looking down
and inward across the compound with the platform now small in the lower corner.
Micro-corrections of 5 to 10 cm throughout so it reads as a flown machine.

ACTION
The platform rises steadily with the cables singing under load. The arrivals
keep their weight on the rail and their eyes on the ground falling away below.
Two of them look up at the rim as it approaches. As the platform crests, wind
catches everyone's clothes at once, and the whole group turns inward together —
the movement passes across the platform like a wave rather than all at once.
Then nobody moves at all. One figure at the rail edge does not turn with the
others; she is already looking down at the compound, and she stays that way as
the camera pulls up and away.

PERFORMANCE
No panic and no crying. The reactions read as young people arriving somewhere
they were told was good: shoulders up, chins lifting, one grin further down the
rail. The stillness that follows the turn is the only tell.

PHYSICS
Steel platform swaying 10 to 15 cm on its cables, cables under real tension and
vibrating, clothing and hair pulled hard sideways at the rim where the wind
crosses. Grip pressure visible in the hands on the rail. Real weight in the
whole assembly.

LIGHTING
Flat overcast-filtered daylight at 5600K from an even sky, no visible sun, no
shadow direction on the white wall face. The compound below sits a stop darker
and cooler at 4000K. Dust haze at 25% on the plain, none on the wall.

AUDIO
Wind rising steadily with altitude, cable tension creaking, the platform's
steel deck flexing under shifting weight, cloth snapping at the rim, one
distant motor working far above. Twenty people breathing and nobody speaking.
No dialogue. No music.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action, 4K, large-format cinema detail, physically real
materials, natural desaturated colour, fine grain, real-time throughout.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The wall stays completely blank — one continuous white surface with no door, no
window, no stair and no opening anywhere in the shot.
Nothing connects the top of the wall to the compound floor except the cables.
EXACTLY ONE platform, carrying the same twenty people from the first frame to
the last.
The compound below stays grey, geometric and empty of people.
Figures stay under one twentieth of frame height once the camera is over the
rim.
This clip is one fragment of a longer film — end mid-move with the camera still
rising, no settling shot.
```
