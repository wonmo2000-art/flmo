```
seen from a slight rise. Rusted tin roofs. Faded laundry strung between two of

where several people sit. Nothing else for miles behind it — cracked open
ground to a soft horizon where a scatter of charred bare tree trunks stands at
intervals.

Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. Flat overcast-filtered midday at

```

```
hair tied back and a wooden walking stick across her knees. A thin
straight-backed old man. An old man with milky blind eyes and perfect posture,
eyes closed. A slightly less grey old man crouched at the end. All of them wear
layered wrapped cloth washed colourless, no armour of any kind. All of them are
entirely at ease. Nobody is looking at anybody.

Photoreal, photographed not illustrated. Flat overcast-filtered midday at
```
