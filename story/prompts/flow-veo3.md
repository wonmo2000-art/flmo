# Flow (Veo 3) 스크립트 — 프롤로그 「눈」

> Google Labs **Flow**용. Seedance 프롬프트와 문법이 다르다.
> [`../script/ep01.md`](../script/ep01.md) 0. 눈 (P1~P49)을 **48초**로 옮긴 것.

## Flow는 Seedance와 이게 다르다

| | Seedance | **Flow (Veo 3)** |
|---|---|---|
| **길이** | 10~30초 | **8초 고정.** 이어붙여서 늘린다 |
| **소리** | 후반에 얹는다 | **같이 생성된다.** 대사·립싱크 포함 |
| **레퍼런스** | 여러 장 | **Ingredients 3장까지** |
| **프롬프트** | 15섹션 장문 | **짧은 한 문단 + Audio 줄.** 길면 희석된다 |

### 반드시 지킬 것 넷

1. **한 컷에 동작 하나.** 8초에 두 가지를 시키면 둘 다 망가진다
2. **대사는 8초에 12~18단어까지.** 넘으면 잘리거나 빨라진다
3. **`no subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text`를 매번 넣는다.** Veo는 자막을 잘 만든다
4. **Ingredients에 어머니 1장 · 아이 1장을 고정으로 넣는다.** 얼굴이 흘러가는 걸 막는 유일한 방법

### 이어붙이는 법

각 컷 끝에서 **Scenebuilder → Extend**를 쓰거나, 앞 컷의 마지막 프레임을
**Frames to Video**에 넣고 다음 프롬프트를 얹는다.
**컷 2·3·4는 Extend로 이어야 얼굴이 안 바뀐다.**

# 프롤로그 · 6컷 · 48초

## FLOW-1 · 비탈 (0:00–0:08)

```
A vast unbroken snow slope rising to a ridge, dark rock breaking through along
the upper left, low cloud erasing the summit. Two tiny figures climb the slope
near the centre of frame, an adult and a child, so small they are almost lost
in it. Wind lifts loose snow off the surface in long horizontal streams across
them. The camera holds still, very wide, and does not move. Flat diffused
overcast light with no sun. Bleached whites and cold greys only. Photoreal
live-action, large-format cinema detail, fine grain.

Audio: heavy wind across open snow, nothing else. No music. No dialogue.

No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.
```

> **첫 컷은 카메라를 안 움직인다.** 8초 동안 산이 그냥 거기 있다.

## FLOW-2 · 손 (0:08–0:16)

```
Close behind an adult and a child climbing a snow slope, seen from just behind
and to the side. Both wear heavy layered fur-and-hide coats crusted pale with
ice, hoods down, dark hair pulled back, and they are holding hands. The child's
long dark braid whips sideways in the wind. Their boots break through the crust
with each step. The camera tracks slowly with them at walking pace, handheld,
staying behind. Flat diffused overcast light, no sun, bleached whites.
Photoreal live-action, fine grain.

Audio: wind, boots breaking through snow crust, fur and hide creaking, two
people breathing hard in cold air. No music. No dialogue.

No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.
```

## FLOW-3 · 미끄러짐 (0:16–0:24)

```
On a steep snow slope, a child in a heavy iced fur coat loses footing and drops
hard onto one knee, sliding a short way. The adult beside them does not let go
of the hand and does not stop walking — the grip holds, the arm goes taut, and
the child is pulled back up onto both feet and keeps moving. Neither of them
looks at the other. The camera stays low and close, handheld, following at
their pace. Flat diffused overcast light, blowing snow. Photoreal live-action,
fine grain.

Audio: a boot losing purchase, a body hitting packed snow, a sharp intake of
breath, wind. No music. No dialogue.

No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.
```

> **이 컷 하나가 어머니의 성격 전부다.** 안 쳐다보고, 안 멈추고, 안 놓는다.

## FLOW-4 · 돌아봄 (0:24–0:32)

```
A child in a heavy iced fur coat stops and looks back over one shoulder while
still walking forward, dark braid across the face, nose and cheeks wind-raw.
Behind them the slope runs away completely empty and their own footprints are
already filling in with blown snow, fading out a few metres back. The child's
expression does not change. The camera is close on the child's face and turns
with the look, handheld. Flat diffused overcast light. Photoreal live-action,
fine grain.

Audio: wind rising, cloth and fur moving, one breath held. No music. No
dialogue.

No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.
```

## FLOW-5 · 능선 (0:32–0:40)

```
Two figures in iced fur coats reach a snow ridge and stop, seen from behind,
an adult and a child holding hands. The camera rises slowly over their
shoulders to reveal what is below: the snow line ends and a vast bleached brown
dust plain runs flat to a soft horizon, charred bare tree trunks standing at
intervals, haze thickening with distance. Far out across the plain, a single
thin white line crosses the entire width of the view. Photoreal live-action,
fine grain.

The child speaks first, small and flat: "저거 뭐예요."
The adult answers without looking down, quiet and even: "학교."

Audio: wind dropping away as they crest the ridge, cloth settling, two voices
in Korean, nothing else. No music.

No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.
```

> **대사는 이 컷에 몰아넣었다.** 카메라가 멈춘 뒤에 말이 나와야 립싱크가 산다.

## FLOW-6 · 손이 펴진다 (0:40–0:48)

```
At the foot of an enormous featureless white wall on cracked dust ground, a
line of people waits, each adult standing with one child. A figure in a clean
grey uniform writes on a slate. The camera pushes slowly in on one pair until
only their two joined hands are in frame, the adult's weathered hand gripping
the child's. The adult's fingers uncurl and open. The child's hand stays where
it was for a moment, then is gone from frame. The camera holds on the empty
open hand. Flat overcast midday, bleached earth against unbroken white.
Photoreal live-action, fine grain.

Before the hand opens, the child asks quietly: "엄마도 같이 가요."
The adult answers, even and immediate: "응."

Audio: dry wind over open ground, dust, cloth, many people standing very
quietly, one slate pencil. Two voices in Korean. No music.

No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.
```

> **"응"이 거짓말이라는 걸 손이 말한다.** 대사 뒤에 손이 펴져야 순서가 맞다.

# 대안 · 4초 파트 4컷

> 액션을 Flow로 테스트하고 싶으면 이쪽. 대사가 거의 없어서 더 안전하다.

## FLOW-A1 · 마당

```
Eight armoured figures in dusty black lacquered plate with exposed silver
joints walk into a small gravel yard between mud-brick houses, spears held
upright, boots loud on the gravel. Chickens scatter around them. In the
background four very old people sit side by side on a low wooden platform
shelling beans and do not look up, not once, the whole time. The camera tracks
in low behind the armoured figures at walking pace. Flat overcast midday,
bleached earth palette. Photoreal live-action, fine grain.

Audio: boots on gravel, armour plates knocking, spear butts, chickens, and
underneath it all a steady quiet tapping of beans being shelled that never
stops. No music. No dialogue.

No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.
```

## FLOW-A2 · 지팡이

```
A small very old woman sits on a low wooden platform in a gravel yard, a wooden
walking stick across her knees, an armoured man standing over her. Without
standing up and without changing her expression she extends the stick once,
straight out. The armoured man folds backwards off his feet and is thrown
across the yard, joints bent wrong, spear spinning away, and hits a mud wall.
Dust bursts flat across the ground. She is still sitting exactly as she was.
The camera stays locked on her, wide enough to see him go. Flat overcast
midday. Photoreal live-action, fine grain.

Audio: one dry impact, armour crumpling, a body hitting packed mud, dust, a
spear clattering on gravel, then quiet. No music. No dialogue.

No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.
```

> **카메라가 그녀를 놓지 않는다.** 날아가는 사람은 프레임 안에서 벌어지는 부수적인 일이다.

## FLOW-A3 · 잔상

```
A gravel yard. Two armoured figures charge at a thin old man sitting on a
wooden platform shelling beans. He is there, and then the space where he was
holds only a smear of hanging dust — he is already back in his seat on the
other side of the platform, shelling another bean, unhurried. The two armoured
figures collide with each other and recoil, helmets knocking. The camera is
locked off and does not move at all. Flat overcast midday. Photoreal
live-action, fine grain.

Audio: running boots, one hard helmet-on-helmet collision, armour hitting
gravel, and a single quiet bean-shelling tap landing after it. No music. No
dialogue.

No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.
```

## FLOW-A4 · 그다음

```
A small gravel yard after a fight. Eight armoured figures lie on the ground in
folded positions, none of them moving and none of them dead. A dented
breastplate, a spear tied into a knot, a person-shaped hole in the mud wall.
Chickens are already walking back in. Behind it all, four very old people sit
on the wooden platform, and one of them is rubbing her knee and grumbling. The
camera pulls slowly back and up into a wide. Flat low afternoon light, long
shadows, bleached earth. Photoreal live-action, fine grain.

An old woman mutters, irritated and to nobody: "아이고 무릎아."

Audio: wind, chickens, armour settling, one old voice in Korean. No music.

No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.
```

> **싸움의 여파가 관절통인 게 이 파트의 전부다.**

## Ingredients 세팅

| 컷 | 넣을 것 |
|---|---|
| **FLOW-1~6** | 어머니 정면 1장 · 아이 정면 1장 · 눈산 1장 |
| **FLOW-A1~A4** | 분이 1장 · 집행자 1장 · 마당 1장 |

**세 장을 넘기지 않는다.** 넘으면 Veo가 섞는다.

## 안 나올 때

| 증상 | 고치는 법 |
|---|---|
| **자막이 나온다** | `no subtitles` 를 프롬프트 **맨 뒤**로 옮긴다 |
| **얼굴이 컷마다 바뀐다** | Extend로 잇는다. 새로 생성하면 무조건 바뀐다 |
| **대사가 빨라진다** | 단어를 줄인다. 8초에 12단어가 안전선 |
| **말이 영어로 나온다** | `Two voices in Korean` 을 Audio 줄 맨 앞으로 |
| **액션이 뭉갠다** | 동작을 하나만 남긴다. FLOW-3처럼 |
| **날아가는 게 예쁘다** | `joints bent wrong` 를 넣는다. 무게가 생긴다 |

## 뽑는 순서

1. **FLOW-1** — 톤이 맞는지. 카메라 안 움직이는 8초가 견딜 만한지
2. **FLOW-5** — 대사와 립싱크가 되는지. **여기가 Flow의 진짜 시험이다**
3. **FLOW-A2** — 액션이 되는지
4. **FLOW-6** — 손이 펴지는 게 읽히는지

**1과 5가 되면 프롤로그 48초를 통으로 뽑을 수 있다.**
