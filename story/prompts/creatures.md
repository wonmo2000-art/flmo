# 크리처 프롬프트 — Seedance 2.0

## 설정: 짐승은 아이들을 공격하지 않는다

아이들의 몸은 방사능으로 가득 차 있다.
그래서 피폭된 짐승들이 그들을 **자기 편으로 알아본다.**

- 아이들이 사막에서 살아남는 이유가 여기서 설명된다
- 그리고 나중에 **진짜 귀수 앞에 섰을 때** 무슨 일이 벌어질지 미리 알려주는 복선이 된다
- → 미결이던 "귀수는 적인가, 교감 가능한 존재인가"에 대한 답의 방향

## 연출 원칙

**짐승을 흉물로 그리지 않는다.**
피폭의 흔적은 남기되 짐승은 존엄하게. 아이들을 대하는 태도와 같다.

빛나게 하지 않는다. 발광은 싸다. 대신 **뿌옇게 먼 눈**, 빠진 털, 켈로이드 흉터.

# 대형 늑대 (大狼)

말만 한 크기. 어깨가 선 어른 키를 넘는다.
잿빛과 뼈흰색 털이 군데군데 빠져 회색 흉터 피부가 드러난다.
눈은 뿌옇게 멀어 초점이 없다. 왼쪽 귀 끝이 없다.

## 2. 늑대와 아이 — 스케일 샷

> **둘 다 서로를 안 보고 카메라를 본다.** 레퍼런스 그림을 무섭게 만드는 지점이 이것이다.

```
Cinematic wide shot, 2.39:1 anamorphic. A vast flat plain of pale dry grass
running back to a low ridge of hills lost in haze, beneath an enormous overcast
sky filling the upper two-thirds of the frame.

Centre frame, an enormous wolf the size of a large horse stands facing camera,
motionless. Ash-grey and bone-white coat fallen away in irregular patches across
shoulders and flank, exposing bare grey keloid-scarred skin. Blackened cracked
muzzle. Clouded pale milky-blue eyes. Left ear missing at the tip.

Standing against its right foreleg, reaching only to the animal's shoulder, a
thin wiry seventeen-year-old East Asian girl with roughly self-cut short black
hair, in a layered bone-white desert wrap of coarse gauze and linen cinched with
worn leather straps, feet bound in cloth. One small pale ceramic port ring at
the base of her throat. She rests one hand flat against the wolf's flank.

Neither of them looks at the other. Both face the camera directly.

cinematic photoreal, anamorphic 2.39:1, large-format, heavy diffusion, flat
overcast light, low contrast, lifted blacks. Pastel-muted grade — bone white,
ash grey, dry straw, powder pink and faint mint in the sky. Fine film grain.
No text, no logo, no watermark.
```

## 3. 무리와 아이들 — 앙상블 와이드

```
Extreme wide cinematic shot, 2.39:1 anamorphic. A vast open plain of pale dry
grass under a huge overcast sky. Five enormous wolves, each the size of a large
horse, stand scattered across the middle distance, ash-grey and bone-white with
patchy fallen coats and bare scarred skin, all facing camera, motionless. Among

small in an immense landscape. Fine film grain. No text, no logo, no watermark.
```

# 귀수 (鬼獸)

> 2026-08-04 4차. 레퍼런스가 설정을 하나 더 던졌다 — **사슬.**

## 사슬 — 누가 누구를 끄는가

사슬은 원래 **상류층이 채운 것**이다. 귀수를 묶어두고 있었다.
그것을 풀어서 손에 쥔 것이 **아이들**이다.

> **같은 사슬, 다른 손.**

아이들이 사막에서 살아남는 이유("짐승은 아이들을 알아본다")가 여기서 값을 치른다.
온 지배계급이 못 하는 것을 아이들만 할 수 있게 된다.

## 디자인 원칙

| | |
|---|---|
| 크기 | 흰 벽(60m)과 대비 가능한 규모. 안개에 먹혀 윤곽만 |
| 뿔 | 거대한 곡선. **프레임을 감싼다** — 프로시니엄 아치처럼 |
| 표면 | 검고 젖은, 깊게 골이 진 가죽 |
| 눈 | **창백한 두 점. 그 외엔 아무것도 빛나지 않는다** |
| 공기 | 먼지가 아니라 **안개.** 차가운 청회색 |

> 늑대에게는 발광을 금지했다. 귀수는 눈만 허용한다.
> 짐승이 아니라 **귀(鬼)**이기 때문이다.

## 몸 속의 사람들

귀수의 가죽 아래에서 **사람 형상이 밀려 올라온다.**
몸, 얼굴, 펼친 손 — 안에서 밀어 올리는 것처럼.
빛이 닿는 각도에서만 보이고, 한 번 봐서는 놓칠 만큼 미묘하게.

→ 괴물이면서 동시에 **무덤**이 된다.
→ 아이들이 저것과 교감할 수 있는 이유가 생긴다. **저 안에 있는 게 자기들의 미래**다.

## 1. 전신 — 스케일

```
Extreme wide cinematic shot, 2.39:1 anamorphic. A colossal horned beast stands in
dense cold fog on a plain of black volcanic rock, its body barely resolved — an
immense dark silhouette, hundreds of metres of it, the shape swallowed by the
moving fog and re-emerging. Two enormous ribbed horns curve up and outward from
its lowered head, arcing across the top third of the frame. Two pale points of
light where its eyes are; nothing else on the creature emits light. Heavy iron
chains, each link the size of a person, run from its neck and forelimbs down into
the rock, hanging slack.

At the very bottom edge of the frame, five tiny figures in bone-white wraps stand
with their backs to camera, facing it.

cinematic photoreal, anamorphic 2.39:1, large-format, flat cold overcast light
diffused through heavy fog, desaturated blue-grey monochrome, deep crushed
blacks. Overwhelming scale, figures almost invisibly small. Fine film grain. No
text, no logo, no watermark.
```

## 2. 사슬 — 대칭 정면

```
Wide cinematic shot, 2.39:1 anamorphic, dead symmetrical composition. Cold
blue-grey fog fills the air. Centre frame, the lowered head of a colossal horned
beast occupies the upper half of the image, its two vast ribbed horns curving
down and outward to frame the composition like a proscenium arch. The face is
dark, wet and almost featureless except for two pale unblinking eyes.

In the lower third, two young women stand with their backs to camera, small
against it, in long bone-white wraps that leave their backs bare — a row of pale
ceramic ports visible down each spine. Each holds a length of heavy iron chain
that runs forward and upward, disappearing into the fog toward the beast.

Neither of them is pulling. Neither is the beast.

cinematic photoreal, anamorphic 2.39:1, large-format, flat diffuse fog light,
desaturated blue-grey, deep blacks, bone-white cloth the only bright value.
Symmetry, immense vertical scale. Fine film grain. No text, no logo, no watermark.
```

## 3. 얼굴 — 귀(鬼)

```
Wide cinematic shot, 2.39:1 anamorphic, low angle, close on the head. The head of
a colossal beast fills the frame, seen through drifting cold fog. Black, wet,
deeply furrowed hide. Two vast ribbed horns sweep out of frame on both sides. Two
pale eyes, the only light in the image.

Across the surface of its hide, faint human shapes press outward from beneath the
skin — the impressions of bodies, faces, open hands, as if pushing up from inside.
They are visible only where the light catches them, subtle enough to be missed on
a first viewing.

cinematic photoreal, anamorphic 2.39:1, large-format, low flat fog light,
desaturated blue-grey and black, deep crushed blacks. Fine film grain. No text,
no logo, no watermark.
```

## 4. 데리고 간다 — 3막용

```
Extreme wide cinematic shot, 2.39:1 anamorphic. A vast plain of pale sand under a
low overcast sky. In the middle distance, five small figures in bone-white wraps
walk away from camera toward the horizon, spread in a loose line — one of them in
a deep red cloak, the only saturated colour in the frame.

Behind them, filling the entire upper half of the image, the colossal horned beast
follows at a walk, its head lowered, vast ribbed horns black against the pale sky.
Heavy iron chains trail slack from its neck across the sand. It is not being
pulled. It is following.

cinematic photoreal, anamorphic 2.39:1, large-format, flat diffuse overcast light,
desaturated pale grade broken only by the red cloak. Overwhelming scale contrast.

```
