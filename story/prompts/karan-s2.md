> S#1의 유일한 대사가 **"보지 마. …아직은, 보지 마."** 였다.
> 그러면 다음 장면은 **아기가 보는 것**이다.

## 이 장면이 하는 일

S#1 대본이 이미 두 가지를 심어놨다.

| 심은 것 | |
|---|---|
| 아기 왼 손목의 **금빛 고리 모양 반점** | |

**둘이 같은 모양이다.** S#2는 그걸 한 프레임에 놓기만 하면 된다.
설명하지 않는다. 나란히 보여주고 끝낸다.

## 대본

**S#2. 사막 — 카란 동쪽 (같은 밤, 계속)**

말발굽 소리. 흰 모래 위를 한 마리가 달린다. 지평선에 붉은 달이 낮게 걸려 있다.
안장 옆 등바구니가 걸음마다 흔들린다. **바구니는 뒤를 향해 매달려 있다.**

바구니 안 — 아기가 운다. 포대기가 흔들린다. 울음이 계속된다.

그러다 그친다.

아기의 눈이 뜨인다. 뒤를 향한 채로.

멀리, 지평선 전체를 가로질러 **검은 벽**이 서 있다. 그 위 새벽 하늘에
불꼬리 하나가 치솟는다. 그리고 터진다.

**거대한 붉은 고리**가 하늘에 그려진다. 지평선의 달과 나란히.
같은 크기, 같은 색. 하늘에 붉은 원이 두 개다.

아기의 검은 눈동자에 그 고리가 통째로 들어와 있다.

포대기 밖으로 나온 왼 손목 — 금빛 고리 모양 반점.
**하늘의 것과 같은 모양이다.**

반점이 아주 잠깐, 한 번 밝아진다.

말은 계속 달린다. 뒤에서 고리가 천천히 타 내린다.
불티가 흰 모래 위로 떨어진다.

아기가 다시 운다.

## 2A · 말

```
SCENE CONTEXT
A single horse runs east across a white sand desert at night, carrying an empty
saddle and a woven basket slung at its side. A deep red moon sits on the
horizon. Far behind, a black wall stands across the whole width of the desert.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@moon — controls the size and surface of the low moon only; it is deep red and
sits on the horizon line.
@karan — controls dune geography and sand material only; reference framing not
inherited. No tents appear in this shot.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: white sand running past the lens, its ripples rose-tinted on the
lit faces and cold blue-white in the shadows. Midground: the horse at full
gallop moving left to right, an empty saddle, a deep woven basket slung against
its flank and hanging so its open side faces backward. Background: flat white
dunes to a horizon, one enormous deep red moon sitting on that horizon at frame
right, and at frame left a matte black wall standing across the desert, already
far away and getting further. Camera is low and running alongside.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Low wide, camera 0.8 metres above the sand and 6 metres out from the horse's
flank, moving with it. The horse fills the centre third. The red moon is at
frame right on the horizon. The black wall occupies the left edge at a
distance of roughly two kilometres, running off the top and bottom of the
frame's left side.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot. The camera does not cut on its own. The run holds one
speed throughout; the drift back to the wall is the only change.

OPTICS
63° FOV for the entire shot, rectilinear, one lens with no change at any point.

CAMERA
Tracking low alongside at 45 km/h with 3 cm tremor, 0.8 metres high, holding
the horse in the centre third for the first half, then easing back and widening
its framing so the black wall enters more of the frame behind. Focus rides the
basket.

ACTION
The horse runs flat out with no rider, reins loose and swinging. The basket
swings against its flank with every stride, its leather draw cords stretched
tight. Sand throws up behind each hoof and hangs in the still air. The black
wall behind does not advance and does not recede on its own — it only gets
smaller because the horse is leaving. The red moon does not move.

PHYSICS
Real horse gait and real mass at gallop. The basket carries weight and swings
on a real pendulum with the stride. Loose reins whip. Sand behaves as dry loose
sand under hooves. The black wall is matte and lightless — the red moon
produces no reflection on it at any angle, and there is no gradient at its
boundary against the sky.

LIGHTING
Night ambient at 8500K, exposed 2 stops under, plus one enormous deep red moon
on the horizon at 1800K as the only colour source. Lit faces of the white dunes
take a rose cast; shaded faces stay cold blue-white. The horse is a dark
silhouette with a red rim along its top line. Haze at 20%.

AUDIO
Hooves at gallop on packed sand, the horse breathing hard, leather cords
creaking, woven basket flexing, wind. An infant crying inside the basket,
present the whole time and rising and falling with the stride. No music. No
dialogue.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action, 4K, large-format cinema detail, physically real
materials, three-value palette of white sand, deep red moonlight and matte
black, fine grain, real-time throughout.

POSITIVE LOCKS
EXACTLY ONE horse and EXACTLY ONE basket in the entire generation. The saddle
stays empty.
The desert stays white sand from the first frame to the last.
The moon stays deep red, on the horizon line, at the same size throughout.
The black wall stays matte, silent and stationary, and returns no reflection.
The basket hangs with its open side facing backward for the entire shot.
This clip is one fragment of a longer film — end mid-gallop, no settling shot.
```

## 2B · 울음이 그친다

```
SCENE CONTEXT
Inside a swinging basket on a running horse, an infant stops crying and opens
its eyes toward what is behind them.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@moon — controls the deep red moon only; it appears out of focus at the frame
edge.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: the woven wall of a deep basket and layered cream and undyed linen
swaddling, close enough to read the weave. Midground: a small face in the
swaddling. Background: completely out of focus — white sand moving past at
speed, a deep red smear at the edge where the moon is. Camera is inside the
basket rim looking down and slightly back.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Extreme close, camera 0.35 metres from the face, framed inside the basket rim
so the woven edge occupies the left quarter. The infant is on its back in the
swaddling with its face turned toward the open side. The whole frame rises and
falls with the horse's stride.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot. The camera does not cut on its own. The crying holds
longest; the silence after it is the shortest and the most important.

OPTICS
29° FOV for the entire shot, rectilinear, one lens with no change at any point.
Shallow depth of field, focus locked on the eyes.

CAMERA
Rigged inside the basket, moving exactly with it — 6 cm vertical bounce at the
stride rate, no independent operator movement at all. Focus stays on the eyes
for the entire shot.

ACTION
The infant is crying hard, face screwed up, both fists closed against the
swaddling, the whole body working with it. The crying continues, ragged and
loud, for most of the shot. Then it stops — not gradually, all at once, between
one breath and the next. The face relaxes. The eyes open and stay open, dark
and wide, and settle on something past the open side of the basket, behind the
running horse. The bouncing continues exactly as before. The eyes do not blink
and do not move again.

PERFORMANCE
The stop is the entire performance beat: crying, then a face at complete rest
with open eyes. No smile. No fear. Nothing readable at all.

PHYSICS
Real infant skin with translucency and fine hair. Real linen weave and real
cloth weight moving with the bounce. The basket flexes audibly with each
stride.

LIGHTING
Night ambient at 8500K, exposed 2 stops under, with deep red 1800K spill
entering from the open side of the basket and falling across one side of the
face, leaving the other in cold blue-white. Out-of-focus background carries a
red smear at the edge. Haze at 20%.

AUDIO
Loud ragged infant crying very close to the microphone, hooves at gallop,
basket weave flexing, leather cords creaking, wind. When the crying stops,
everything else continues at exactly the same level — the absence is the event.
Every sound is unpitched and textural — no tones, no drones, no sustained notes, no instruments, no singing. No dialogue.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action, 4K, large-format cinema detail, physically real
materials, fine grain, real-time throughout.

POSITIVE LOCKS
EXACTLY ONE infant in the entire generation.
The bounce rate stays identical before and after the crying stops.
The eyes open and stay open and fixed for the rest of the shot.
The background stays fully out of focus.
This clip is one fragment of a longer film — end on the open eyes, no settling
shot.
```

## 2C · 하늘의 두 원

```
SCENE CONTEXT
Seen from a fleeing horse, a burning arrow climbs above a distant black wall
and bursts into a red ring of fire, level with a red moon already sitting on
the horizon.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@moon — controls the size and surface of the low moon only; it is deep red and
sits on the horizon line at frame right.
@karan — controls dune geography and sand material only; reference framing not
inherited.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: white sand at the bottom edge, moving fast right to left. Midground:
open white dune field falling away behind. Background: a matte black wall
standing across the entire width of the desert roughly two kilometres away,
running from the sand to about a third of frame height, and above it the
pre-dawn sky. The deep red moon sits on the horizon at frame right, clear of
the wall. Camera looks backward from a moving vantage.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Wide, camera 1.6 metres above the sand, looking back along the direction of
travel, the whole frame drifting right to left with the horse's speed. The
black wall occupies the lower third from edge to edge. The red moon sits on the
horizon at frame right at the same height as the top of the wall. The sky above
is empty.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot. The camera does not cut on its own. The empty sky holds
longest; the climb is fastest; the ring holds again at the end.

OPTICS
84° FOV for the entire shot, rectilinear, one lens with no change at any point.

CAMERA
Rear-facing from the moving horse at 45 km/h with 4 cm tremor, 1.6 metres high,
drifting steadily right to left. It tilts up 20 degrees to follow the arrow and
then holds. One axis only, no pan, no push.

ACTION
The black wall stands still across the horizon and the desert keeps sliding
past. A single point of fire leaves the top of the wall and climbs, drawing a
clean trail behind it, slowing as it rises. At altitude it bursts — the fire
opens outward into one continuous closed ring, deep red, and hangs there
burning. It settles at the same apparent width as the moon and at the same
height above the horizon. For the rest of the shot there are two red circles in
the sky, side by side, the same size and the same colour. Embers drift down
from the ring toward the white sand, very slowly. The wall does not move.

PHYSICS
The arrow follows a real ballistic climb and loses speed as it rises. The fire
trail behaves as combustion in moving air. The ring expands from a point, finds
its diameter, holds shape and burns down from its inner edge. Embers fall with
real air resistance. The black wall stays matte and returns no reflection of
either the moon or the ring.

LIGHTING
Night ambient at 8500K, exposed 2 stops under, with the deep red moon at 1800K
as the only source until the burst. After the burst, a second 1800K source of
the same colour lifts the whole white dune field by a stop and a half from
behind, throwing long shadows toward camera. The black wall stays black and
takes no light from either. Haze at 20% and both circles light it from above.

AUDIO
Hooves at gallop, wind, basket weave, the horse breathing. From far behind, a
faint rising whistle, then one full second of silence, then a deep soft
concussion that rolls across the dunes and arrives late. The low unpitched rumble that
was coming from the black wall stops entirely at the burst. No music. No
dialogue.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action, 4K, large-format cinema detail, physically real
materials, three-value palette of white sand, deep red light and matte black,
fine grain, real-time throughout.

POSITIVE LOCKS
EXACTLY ONE arrow and EXACTLY ONE ring of fire in the entire generation.
The ring is a single continuous closed circle and reads at the same width, the
same colour and the same height above the horizon as the moon — the sky holds
two identical red circles for the rest of the shot.
The moon stays on the horizon line at the same size throughout.
The black wall stays matte, still and lightless.
The camera moves on one axis only.
This clip is one fragment of a longer film — end with the ring still burning
and embers still falling, no settling shot.
```

## 2D · 같은 모양

```
SCENE CONTEXT
An infant's hand is out of its wrapping inside a swinging basket. The ring mark
on its wrist and the ring of fire in the sky behind are the same shape.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@moon — controls the deep red moon only; it appears out of focus in the
background.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: cream and undyed linen swaddling and a small open hand, close
enough to read the skin. Midground: the woven basket rim. Background: fully out
of focus — the night sky with two red circles in it, one on the horizon and one
above the dark band of the wall, both reduced to soft red discs. Camera is
inside the basket, low, looking along the arm toward the open side.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Extreme close, camera 0.3 metres from the wrist, framing from the elbow to the
fingertips with the basket rim across the bottom. The two soft red discs sit in
the upper background, out of focus and clearly the same size as each other. The
whole frame rises and falls with the stride.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot. The camera does not cut on its own. The hold on the mark
is the whole shot.

OPTICS
18° FOV for the entire shot, rectilinear, one lens with no change at any point.
Shallow depth of field with the background fully dissolved.

CAMERA
Rigged inside the basket, moving exactly with it — 6 cm vertical bounce at the
stride rate, no independent operator movement. Focus locked on the inside of
the wrist for the entire shot.

ACTION
The small hand is open and still. On the inside of the left wrist there is a
mark shaped like a closed ring, the colour of old gold, slightly raised, with
clean edges, reading as part of the skin. The two red discs sit behind it,
softly out of focus and unmistakably the same shape. Nothing moves except the
bounce. Then the mark brightens once — a single slow rise and fall over about
one second, warm and even, lighting the linen immediately around it and nothing
further. The hand does not react. The fingers stay open. The bounce continues
unchanged.

PHYSICS
Real infant skin with translucency, fine hair and visible fine creases at the
wrist. Real linen weave. The glow behaves as light with a real falloff — it
reaches the linen within two centimetres and stops.

LIGHTING
Night ambient at 8500K, exposed 2 stops under, with deep red 1800K spill
entering from the open side of the basket. During the brightening, one small
warm source at the wrist itself, the only light in the frame that is not red.
Haze at 20%.

AUDIO
Hooves at gallop, basket weave flexing, linen very close to the microphone,
wind, the horse breathing. Far behind, the last of the concussion still rolling
away. No infant crying during the brightening. It starts again in the final
moment. Every sound is unpitched and textural — no tones, no drones, no sustained notes, no instruments, no singing. No dialogue.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action, 4K, large-format cinema detail, physically real
materials, fine grain, real-time throughout.

POSITIVE LOCKS
EXACTLY ONE ring-shaped mark, on the inside of the left wrist, holding the same
size, shape and colour for the entire shot.
The mark brightens exactly once and its light reaches only the linen beside it.
The two red discs stay in the background, out of focus, the same size as each
other and the same shape as the mark.
The hand stays open and still throughout.
This clip is one fragment of a longer film — end as the crying starts again, no
settling shot.
```

## 이 장면의 규칙 넷

**1. 아무도 설명하지 않는다.**
반점과 하늘의 고리가 같은 모양이라는 걸 **말하지 않는다.** 한 프레임에 넣고 끝낸다.
2D가 이 장면의 전부이고, 대사가 하나도 없다.

**2. 울음이 그치는 게 사건이다.**
2B에서 소리가 커지는 게 아니라 **하나만 사라진다.** 말발굽도 바구니 소리도 그대로다.
그래서 없어진 게 뭔지 정확히 들린다.

**3. 하늘에 붉은 원이 두 개다.**
2C의 유일한 목표다. 크기·색·지평선으로부터의 높이까지 같게 락을 걸었다.
**"보지 마"라고 했는데, 아기가 보고 있다.**

**4. 검은 벽은 끝까지 빛을 안 먹는다.**
불의 고리가 사막 전체를 한 스톱 반 들어올려도 **벽만 검다.**
이게 저것이 구름이 아니라는 마지막 증거다.
