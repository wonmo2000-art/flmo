- **Swap the weapon freely.** The blade is an assumption — the second reference
  carries no weapon. Replace the two blade lines in ACTIVE REFERENCES and CUT 3

  and a torch-bearing warband; the second is a turnaround sheet with five

  keep both passages if the prompt is trimmed. If a batch still renders extra

  ground line, marked "her head at its knee" — and attach it as a third
  reference.
